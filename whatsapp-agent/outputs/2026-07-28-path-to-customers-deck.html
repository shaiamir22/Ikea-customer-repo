07  /  Commercial shape

Two models. I want us to pick which one we open with.

Fixed-scope
“Phase 2 — Go Public”

One priced project. Closed scope, defined success metrics, gated on the December review.

For

Cleanest to invoice against given five months unpaid. Keeps the ₪10k strictly maintenance. Forces IKEA to define what “public launch” actually means.

Against

Scope negotiation takes time we may not have before December.

Time & materials
partnership

Bill the dynamic work as it happens, under a monthly cap.

For

No renegotiation per feature. Matches how the work actually behaves. The direction the 22/7 refinement session leaned toward.

Against

Weakest leverage on the arrears — open-ended billing to a client who hasn't paid is a hard sell internally. Needs a cap to be acceptable.

Either way, two things hold: the retainer DoD goes in writing, and the eight G3 items are paid scope, not retainer.
