What people ask the agent — and how they react

IKEA Israel WhatsApp agent · 258 production turns · 4 users · 2026-05-16 → 05-18

Every figure on this page is computed from two corpora extracted from production S3 traces (trajectories.jsonl, mined_queries.jsonl). Every quote is verbatim user text. Nothing is modelled, estimated, or LLM-summarised.

⚠ These four users are testers running scripted QA, not customers

18% of turns are שיחה חדשה resets, and the kitchen script replays 12 times in one morning on one phone with near-identical wording. So topic frequencies and demand rankings on this page are not generalisable. What is generalisable is the failure mechanics — the pipeline cannot tell a tester from a customer typing the same Hebrew sentence. Read this as a behaviour-and-defect study, not market research.

The loop: the bot narrows instead of answering (34%, half of it forced by a validator). The user replies tersely — 5, לבן, מתכת — and the original constraint does not travel with the reply: 16% of the 74 terse agent turns returned zero results. The user rephrases; search_cap and blocked_repeat_query now block the retry. The turn ends in a text-only apology, and the user resets.

2 · Qualitative — what people actually said

12 turns (4.7%) are the user telling us, unprompted, that we failed. These are self-labelled defects, and they are worth more than any satisfaction score because they name what broke. A regex over ~15 Hebrew pushback patterns recovered all 12 with no false positives.

Three of those — metal/wood, "the style I asked for earlier", and the kitchen warranty — are users noticing that the bot dropped a constraint they had already given. That is mechanisms M3 and M4 being observed from the outside, by the customer, in plain Hebrew.

One user asked for a product photo eleven consecutive times over eight minutes. Only 3 of 11 turns routed to show_images. This defect does not appear on the open-issues list in PROJECT-STATUS.md.

They are complements, and one half is missing. The quantitative side is fully built and running on a live pipeline. The qualitative side — the only signal that carries severity and customer impact — is not instrumented at all. The pushback regex closes that gap for about half a day of work, with no LLM and no table scan.

The cross-segment pattern is the finding: the zero-result rate rises as behaviour gets more exploratory — 19% for the scripted and series-name-driven users, 25–30% for the two who described what they wanted in their own words. 5 · Caveats — read before quoting anything

  • The 61% zero-search figure is partly cap-contaminated. search_cap and search_cat_cap fired 3–5× on each failing turn, and a blocked search reports zero rows without proving the query would have failed. Only the first two searches per turn are clean evidence. The defensible turn-level figure is 25 of 123 searching turns (20%).
  • Dedup was ruled out on the failing turns — session_before.shown_item_nos was empty and the cart was empty, so these are not dedup-blocks.
  • A possible regression, not asserted. search(query="סיר לילה") returned 2 real SKUs (LILLA, LOCKIG) on 05-16, then zero on 05-18 for the identical string on a freshly reset session. Both were first-or-second searches, so neither is cap-contaminated. Two data points is not a trend — replay it (scripts/debug_trace.py --text "סיר לילה") to settle it.
  • May data, v4.x era. prompt_version is None throughout this sample — an instrumentation gap that means no finding here can be tied to a prompt version. The product is now on v4.9.11. Every defect needs re-verification against today's index before it is acted on.
  • n = 4 users. Segments are descriptive of these four people. They are a hypothesis about behaviour types worth testing at 1,500 users — not a validated persona set.

6 · What to do with this

  • Ship the Hebrew pushback detector — half a day, no LLM, no scan, backfillable over all history. It is the only continuous quality metric grounded in what customers actually complain about.
  • Re-verify the image failure before the release. Highest-severity user-visible defect here and absent from the open-issues list.
  • Replay the zero-result clusters — kids' towels, potty training, style search, numeric dimensions, bathroom vanities, Hebrew-transliterated series. ~2 hours, and it settles the regression question.
  • Fix the typo'd reset. יחה חדשה (dropped letter) and שיחה חדשהֿ (stray diacritic) both missed the reset pattern and burned a full LLM turn. 18% of turns are resets.
  • Stamp prompt_version into the trajectory record so the next analysis can attribute findings to a version.
  • Re-run this on the first week of 1,500-employee traffic, where the topic mix will finally mean something.
